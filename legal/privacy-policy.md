## 2. What Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data you give us directly
- **Email address** — when you sign up for an account or purchase a subscription
- **Payment information** — collected by Lemon Squeezy (our payment processor). We never receive or store your card number

### 2.2 Data Atlas collects automatically during use
- **Licence key** — transmitted to our licence server once per day for session token refresh and tier verification
- **Project ID** — a short string you choose (e.g. "my-project") transmitted at ingest time to enforce the free-tier project limit. We do not receive your project file paths or source code
- **Tool call counts** — anonymous counts of which Atlas tools were used (atlas_ingest, atlas_query, etc.) for usage analytics. No code content is included

### 2.3 Data we do NOT collect
- **Your source code** — Atlas indexes your codebase locally. No source code, file contents, function bodies, or variable names are ever transmitted to our servers
- **Browsing data** — we do not use tracking pixels or cross-site tracking

## 6. Your Rights

### Under POPIA (South African users):
- **Access** — request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- **Correction** — request correction of inaccurate information
- **Deletion** — request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Objection** — object to processing based on legitimate interest
- **Complaints** — lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at www.justice.gov.za/inforeg/

### Under GDPR (EEA/UK users):
- All rights above, plus:
- **Portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Restriction** — request we restrict processing in certain circumstances
- **Complaints** — lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@hornsoftwarearchitects.co.za. We will respond within 30 days.

## 7. Security

We protect personal information using:
- TLS encryption for all data in transit
- Ed25519 cryptographic signing for licence keys
- Licence server access restricted by API key
- No plaintext passwords stored (authentication via Lemon Squeezy)

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the relevant regulator within the timeframe required by applicable law.
